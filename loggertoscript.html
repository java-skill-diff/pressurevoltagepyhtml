Acquisition settings

The NI-DAQ (Dev1/ai0) and Agilent 34401A (VISA/USB) cannot be reached from a browser, so they are simulated. The rough gauge can use the real RS-232 port through Web Serial (Chrome/Edge). Fixed settings: 1 s sample interval, 1200 baud, command #01RD\r.
